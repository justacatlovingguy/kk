INVESTIGATION DESIGN

Digital Forensic Investigations

Walk through a defensive evidence review with a timeline, competing explanations, and explicit uncertainty.

Preserve and document

Set an authorized scope. Record where each item came from, who handled it, when it was collected, and how its integrity was checked. Keep evidence in approved storage and work from authorized copies.

Compare explanations

Normalize timestamps while retaining the original time zone. Track observations and inferences separately. Compare each plausible explanation against the available evidence and document the limits of the finding.

Illustrative case

A file appears to have disappeared. An audit export and a backup index show different timestamps. One explanation is a time-zone mismatch, and another is that the records describe different events. The reviewer identifies the missing record that would help resolve the timeline.
