WORKFLOW DESIGN

IT Service Delivery

Follow a support request from intake and prioritization through verification, closure, and documentation.

Intake and triage

Capture the request, affected users, urgency, business impact, and what changed. Distinguish a service request from an incident, then propose an owner, priority, and acknowledgment for human review.

Work and verification

Use an approved runbook or escalate with a reproducible symptom and relevant observations. Record what was tried and verify recovery through the user’s normal workflow. The technician approves changes and confirms closure.

Illustrative case

A team reports that its shared printer is unavailable. The intake establishes whether the issue affects one device or the whole site. The assigned technician verifies a successful print from an affected workstation and records the resolution and any follow-up.
