ARCHITECTURE V1.5

Claim review

V1.5 adds a private submission route for new cases. The lab displays published, human-reviewed cases. The same curated case powers Review mode and the Save Our AI Society vote-and-reveal exercise.

Market patterns

  • Claim navigation and flags draw on Typeahead and GetSolved.
  • Question context and scope draw on HowToAI.
  • Source existence and claim support are tracked separately, following patterns in TextSight and GPTZero.
  • Olostep provides a comparator for three-way verdicts with sources.

Evidence policy

Yes means evidence supports the exact claim. No means evidence contradicts it. Maybe means the evidence is incomplete or conflicting and the case specifies a next check.

Run a Society session

Choose Society mode, vote independently, then compare the vendor passages. Discuss why the Typeahead quota claim is contradicted and what would resolve GetSolved’s free full-document allowance. Turn a confirmed error into a review rule and retest it on a new claim.

From submission to register

Visitors can paste an AI answer and identify a claim to check. A reviewer checks the sources and publishes selected cases after removing identifying details and reviewing publication permission. Submissions do not appear automatically.

Release scope

The browser renders versioned cases with human-reviewed outcomes. Votes stay in page memory. Live extraction, web retrieval, and document uploads are candidates for V2.0.

Investigation continuation

A website, app or QR entry can open the same intake. When a visitor opts in, the proposed live service would save the original question, reviewed answer, sources and open points under an investigation ID.

A text, email or QR code would lead to that investigation after sign-in and an ownership check. Revocable links can expire independently of the saved investigation. Follow-ups attach to the same case, with a new revision when evidence changes the answer.

The v1.6 public demo encodes only the current fictional sample step in its URL.
