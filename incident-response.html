Security Incident Response

See how a team can prepare roles, approvals, communications, and recovery checks before an incident.

Prepare the response

Define response ownership, severity thresholds, escalation contacts, and who can approve containment or restoration. Keep a decision log and a communications schedule.

Respond and recover

Record observations and impact, assign an incident lead, and preserve material needed for review. Have the authorized team choose containment measures, verify recovery criteria, and monitor for recurrence. AI can summarize reports and highlight missing information for the lead.

Illustrative exercise

A service owner reports an unexpected configuration change. The lead assigns responsibility, documents the available observations, and schedules an update. The team confirms the approved configuration and service health before closing the exercise.
